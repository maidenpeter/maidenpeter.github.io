Employer data stays with the employer. Each worked example below runs the role's core skill on public data instead.

2024 to 2025
Local and regional market

Reserving Analyst, MUA Mauritius and East Africa

  • Group-level IBNR reserve calculations across multiple entities
  • Reserve adequacy testing on the reserving methodology itself
  • IFRS 17 reporting support

Worked example on public data: a chain ladder fitted on what was known at the end of 1994 and held against the next three years of payments. Five of six US industry lines were over-reserved by 6 to 13%; private motor came in at 0.867 of expected. See also the IFRS 17 measurement case study.

2023 to 2024
Local market

Actuarial Analyst, MUA Insurance (Kenya) Limited

  • Product and claims analysis to support pricing decisions
  • Capital modelling, stress testing and scenario analysis
  • Portfolio, intermediary, and risk and profitability analysis

Worked example on public data: Buhlmann-Straub credibility across 74 US private motor insurers. An insurer needs about US$418m of five-year premium before its own experience earns half credibility; for the smaller half of insurers the blend predicted the next loss ratios 11.0% more accurately than their own experience. See also the Kenya Loss Map rate gap.

Products

Screenshots are on the home page.

AgroShield

Parametric maize insurance for Kenyan smallholders, sold through SACCOs: a rainfall index triggers payouts and every pricing run can be reproduced. Shown on a fictional insurer with illustrative pricing; M-Pesa payment is simulated in the demo.

Mapema

Forecasts severe pastoral drought one to three months ahead for Kenya's northern pastoral counties, and turns the forecast into a household decision in shillings and an SMS in Kiswahili.

Matchday Model

A Dixon-Coles football model and the pipeline that turns real match data into pre-match probabilities, a most likely scoreline and an accuracy scorecard after the game.
