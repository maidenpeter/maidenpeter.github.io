Method

From the regulator's files to a finding

A return can add up on its own and still be wrong, so every company is also read across its quarters. Errors in the official tables are documented, not hidden.

Collect165 files

Every IRA statistical publication online from 2008, catalogued with source links and hashes.

Extract233,685 rows

Revenue accounts and class tables from workbooks whose layouts and company names shift every year.

Validate42,246 checks

Identities, class totals, IRA's printed totals and a cross-check against the PDF releases.

Flag12 returns

Returns that add up but cannot be right, such as year-to-date premium that falls, kept out of quarterly totals.

Analyse4 pages

Profitability, market structure, a reinsurer's scorecard and an explorer, each reproducible with one command.

Code

Three repositories, one pipeline

Python and pandas, tests run on every push, and every published number rebuilt from the raw files.

ira-kenya-data

The data layer: extraction, company crosswalk, single-quarter panels, quality flags and validation.

ira-kenya-insurance

The insurer's view: class profitability, rate adequacy, reserving warnings and market structure.

ira-kenya-reinsurance

The reinsurer's view: a cedant scorecard on four pillars, with a back-test and sensitivity tests.

Source: Insurance Regulatory Authority, Kenya, quarterly industry statistics (unaudited returns). Everything here is public data; nothing comes from inside any insurer. Grades and rankings are relative screening measures, not credit ratings.
