01Featured work

Kenya insurance series

Every general insurer and reinsurer in Kenya, every quarter from Q4 2016 to Q1 2026, extracted from the Insurance Regulatory Authority's statistics and checked against itself. Four pages, one validated dataset, three public repositories.

Motor lost money in every one of the eight years from 2018 to 2025. Every other class together made KSh 19.6bn.

How the series is built
Profitability

Kenya Insurance Loss Map

Motor lost KSh 52.6bn on underwriting from 2018 to 2025, in every one of the eight years. On 2023 to 2025 experience, motor commercial premiums would need to rise 15.6% to break even.

Data
IRA quarterly returns, full years 2018 to 2025, 14 classes, every general insurer.
Method
Class results by year; break-even rate change by the loss ratio method; Fisher's exact test on motor books that look too good.
Open the pageCode: ira-kenya-insurance
Reinsurance

Kenya Cedant Scorecard

Every insurer graded A to E each year the way a reinsurer screens a cedant. 3 of the 5 insurers placed under statutory management or liquidation since 2022 were in the bottom fifth a year before the regulator acted.

Data
IRA balance sheets, revenue accounts and return-quality flags, 2016 to 2025.
Method
Peer percentiles on four pillars (balance sheet, operations, business profile, return quality), a capital cap, a back-test and weight sensitivity.
Open the pageCode: ira-kenya-reinsurance
Market structure

Kenya Insurance Market Structure

In 2020, two insurers wrote over KSh 10bn a year and held 16% of the market. By 2025, seven did, holding 53%. The ten insurers losing share ran a median combined ratio of 112.0%.

Data
Written premium and results for every general insurer, 2016 to 2025, pro forma for mergers.
Method
Herfindahl-Hirschman index, share shifts, growth against combined ratio, and expense rates measured class for class against the five largest insurers.
Open the pageCode: ira-kenya-insurance
Data

Kenya Insurer Explorer

Any insurer, any year: premium, ratios, single quarters, class mix, balance sheet and every return-quality flag, including the 12 returns that add up on their own but cannot be right.

Data
21,900 revenue-account rows and 211,785 class rows from 165 IRA files catalogued back to 2008.
Method
Extraction from shifting workbook layouts, a name crosswalk across renames and mergers, single quarters from year-to-date figures, 42,246 checks.

Health IBNR Lab

A synthetic medical book of 64,646 claims. Best estimate outstanding KES 138,821,204, Mack CV 12.0%. Then the test that matters: a 3-month, 30% payment slowdown makes chain ladder under-reserve by 50.0%.

Method
Paid and received triangles, chain ladder, Mack, Bornhuetter-Ferguson, an ODP bootstrap and an IFRS 17 liability for incurred claims view.
Data
Seeded synthetic portfolio. Illustrative only.

Instalment Persistency Lab

Pay-as-you-go health cover on a synthetic book of 20,000 policies. 57.3% are still in force after the 10th instalment, and members who lapse are paid KES 1.14 in claims for every KES 1 they paid in, against KES 0.65 for members who stay.

Method
Decrement tables in SQL (DuckDB), survival checks in R, an early warning model and a product levers simulator.

Discounting makes a reserve smaller. On one line, IFRS 17 makes it bigger

Read the case study
Data

Method

06How I work

A total that balances is the first thing I check

The habit from actuarial training that stuck: distrust a clean number until it survives being split apart.

  • Rebuild before analysingA regulator's table is a secondary source. Every IRA table was rebuilt from its parts and matched to the printed total before any finding was drawn from it.
  • Read across quarters as well as down the pageA return can add up and still be wrong. Twelve IRA returns pass every identity but fail when read against the same insurer's other quarters, so they are flagged and kept out of quarterly totals.
  • Flag, never deleteExcluded rows stay in the data with a flag, so any exclusion can be reversed and audited by someone else.
  • Say what it cannot tell youEach page names its data and method before its first finding and ends with its limits. Grades and rankings are screening measures, not credit ratings.
  • Make it rerunOne command rebuilds each repository from the raw files, and GitHub Actions runs the tests on every push.
  • Show my own errorsWhen a check catches a mistake of mine, the fix and what it changed are written up, not quietly patched.
