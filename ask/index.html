Ask the data

Every answer is calculated from the validated IRA panel. No language model, so nothing is made up.

How this works

Pick a question, or type one. Your browser loads a compact file of derived figures, built and tested from the validated panel, and calculates the answer from it: the finding, its numbers, a chart, the source and the page that shows it in full. Each answer lets you change its insurer, class or year and suggests what to ask next. A typed question is matched to the nearest question here, and nothing is calculated until you confirm it.

Method

How the answers are calculated

Each answer is a lookup, a sum, a rank or a ratio of the figures below, done in your browser. The same answers are recomputed in Python straight from the processed tables, and read back from this page in a browser; any difference fails the site's checks.

Full year
The fourth-quarter return, year to date. 2017 is left out because IRA has no Q4 2017 workbook online. ira-kenya-data, README.md
Market
Insurers only, micro insurers included, as IRA counts the market. Reinsurers are left out. ira-kenya-insurance, src/profitability/build.py
Written premium
Gross direct premium plus inward reinsurance. ira-kenya-data, README.md
Net earned premium
Net premium written plus reserves brought forward less reserves carried forward. ira-kenya-data, docs/data_dictionary.md
Incurred claims
Net incurred claims. ira-kenya-data, docs/data_dictionary.md
Underwriting result
Net earned premium less claims, commissions and expenses. ira-kenya-data, docs/data_dictionary.md
Investment income
Investment income allocated to general business. ira-kenya-data, docs/data_dictionary.md
Loss ratio
Incurred claims over net earned premium, where earned premium is positive. ira-kenya-insurance, src/profitability/build.py
Combined ratio
Claims, commissions and management expenses over net earned premium. ira-kenya-data, explorer/template.html
Market share
Written premium over the total of every insurer with positive written premium. ira-kenya-insurance, src/structure/build.py
Class
The 13 classes of the Loss Map; Motor Commercial PSV is counted in Motor Commercial. ira-kenya-insurance, src/profitability/build.py
Grade
Score bands: A 70 and above, B 55 to 70, C 40 to 55, D 25 to 40, E below 25. Negative equity, or equity under IRA's minimum capital, caps the grade at D. ira-kenya-reinsurance, src/scorecard/score.py
Bottom fifth
A rank below 80% of the insurers scored that year. ira-kenya-reinsurance, src/scorecard/backtest.py
Reserve
A volume-weighted chain ladder on the paid triangle: the projected ultimate less what has been paid. maidenpeter.github.io, tools/build.py

The figures file (163 KB) holds, for each insurer, its full-year totals, the four class figures the Insurer Explorer already shows (gross premium, net earned premium, incurred claims and underwriting result), its scorecard results, and the six US reserves. The extracted tables themselves are not published here. Built from ira-kenya-data at commit 562b41c773eb and ira-kenya-insurance at commit 7578c0edb6b6 and ira-kenya-reinsurance at commit 08a9d6b09c5e.

An answer that names an insurer reads its unaudited returns to the IRA. A screening view, not a credit opinion.
